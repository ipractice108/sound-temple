# Sound Temple Bali - Luxury Eco-Retreat Website

A modern, SEO-optimized website for Sound Temple, a luxury eco-retreat in Nuanu Creative City, Bali.

## Project Overview

Sound Temple is a premium retreat destination offering:
- Luxury eco-accommodation (4 room types)
- Transformative wellness experiences
- Creative workshops and assessor services
- Farm-to-table dining
- Sustainable living practices

## Website Structure

### Pages
1. **index.html** - Home page with hero, features, and room preview
2. **about.html** - Vision, philosophy, team, and Nuanu Creative City info
3. **rooms.html** - 4 accommodation options with detailed features and pricing
4. **experiences.html** - Wellness programs, creative workshops, assessor services, retreat packages
5. **dining.html** - Temple Kitchen menu, beverages, cooking classes, special experiences
6. **blog.html** - Stories, articles, and newsletter subscription
7. **contact.html** - Contact form, location info, FAQ

## Content Guidelines

### Writing Style
- Concise business English
- Simple, clear sentences
- Active voice preferred
- No hype words
- Focus on benefits
- Professional yet warm tone

### Avoid
- Revolutionary, game-changing
- Best, ultimate, perfect
- Overuse of "luxury"
- Marketing clichés

### Use
- Transform, create, discover
- Sanctuary, haven, space
- Authentic, sustainable, mindful
- Experience, journey, practice
