Handpan Training

Learn to play this mesmerizing instrument with expert guidance.

Description

One of the largest handpan academies with years of teaching experience.

Here you can explore a wide collection of handpans, learn from professional teachers, and begin your journey with the instrument.

We have trained thousands of students and developed our own teaching systems — combining rest, creativity, and skill-building into one experience.

You will not only relax, but also leave with practical musical skills.

What You'll Learn

  • Basic handpan playing techniques
  • Rhythm and timing fundamentals
  • Melodic patterns and composition
  • Hand positioning and tone control
  • Creative improvisation
  • Instrument care and maintenance

Training Formats

Group Training
Perfect for beginners and social learners. Small groups of 4-6 students create a supportive environment for learning together.

Individual Training
One-on-one sessions tailored to your skill level and goals. Personalized attention and faster progression.

VIP Mentorship
Intensive training with master teachers. Advanced techniques, performance preparation, and custom composition support.

What's Included

  • Access to our handpan collection
  • Professional instruction
  • Teaching materials and guides
  • Recording of your sessions available
  • Practice time with instruments
  • Certificate upon completion
