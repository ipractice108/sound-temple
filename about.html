Our Vision

Creating a space where luxury meets sustainability, creativity flows naturally, and transformation begins.

Who We Are

Sound Temple is more than a retreat — it's a sanctuary for creators, seekers, and dreamers. Located in Nuanu Creative City, we've built a space that honors Bali's natural beauty while providing modern comfort and inspiring experiences.

We understand that true transformation happens when you're surrounded by the right environment. That's why every detail at Sound Temple is designed to support your journey, whether you're here to create, heal, or simply reconnect with yourself.

Our retreat combines the best of Bali's wellness traditions with contemporary design and sustainable practices. We're not just offering accommodation — we're providing a complete ecosystem for personal and creative growth.

From our eco-conscious architecture to our carefully curated experiences, everything at Sound Temple reflects our commitment to mindful luxury.

Our Philosophy

Four principles guide everything we do

Sustainable Luxury Without Compromise

We believe luxury and sustainability go hand in hand. Our eco-friendly practices never compromise on comfort or style. From solar power to locally-sourced materials, every choice reflects our commitment to the planet.

Authentic Connection to Balinese Culture

We honor Bali's rich cultural heritage through partnerships with local artisans, traditional ceremonies, and authentic experiences. Our retreat is a bridge between ancient wisdom and modern living.

Personal Transformation Through Experience

Real change comes from immersive experiences, not passive observation. Our programs are designed to actively engage you in practices that foster growth, creativity, and self-discovery.

Community-Driven Creativity

Being part of Nuanu Creative City means being surrounded by artists, innovators, and conscious creators. We foster connections that inspire collaboration and lasting friendships.
