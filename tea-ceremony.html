Tea Ceremony

Mindful tea ceremony in the sound dome.

Description

A mindful tea ceremony hosted inside our unique sound dome, where every sip is amplified by the resonant architecture.

This is more than drinking tea — it's a meditation, a pause, a moment of complete presence. The sound dome creates an atmosphere where taste, aroma, and sensation become deeply vivid.

We use premium organic teas from around the world, prepared with intention and served in silence or with gentle acoustic accompaniment.

The Experience

Each tea ceremony is an invitation to slow down and taste with full awareness. The mirrored walls of the sound dome reflect not just light, but the subtle energies of the practice itself.

  • Welcome and intention setting
  • Brief history of tea ceremony traditions
  • Mindful tea preparation demonstration
  • Guided meditation while tea is brewing
  • Silent tea drinking practice
  • Optional gentle sound accompaniment
  • Closing reflection and sharing

Tea Selection

We rotate our tea offerings seasonally. Current selection includes:

  • Japanese Matcha - ceremonial grade
  • Chinese Pu-erh - aged and earthy
  • Taiwanese Oolong - floral and complex
  • White Peony - delicate and subtle
  • Herbal infusions - caffeine-free options

Benefits

  • Cultivates mindfulness and presence
  • Slows down mental activity
  • Enhances sensory awareness
  • Creates space for reflection
  • Connects with ancient traditions
  • Natural relaxation without effort

What to Expect

The ceremony takes place in silence or with minimal guidance. You'll sit on comfortable cushions in the sound dome, where temperature and lighting are optimized for relaxation. The entire experience is designed to be effortless — simply be present and receive.
