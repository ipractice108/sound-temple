How to Find Us

From Ngurah Rai Airport

60 minutes by car

Take the coastal road north through Canggu, then head inland toward Tabanan. Sound Temple is located within Nuanu Creative City.

Airport Transfer Service
We offer private transfers: $45 per vehicle (up to 3 guests)
Book when confirming your reservation.

From Ubud

30 minutes by car

Head west toward Tabanan. Nuanu Creative City is well-signposted on the main road. Easy access to cultural sites and rice terraces.

Local Transportation
Taxi and ride-sharing apps work well. We can also arrange private drivers for day trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in room rates?

All rooms include daily breakfast, morning yoga session, high-speed WiFi, housekeeping, access to communal facilities, and a welcome package. Additional meals and experiences can be booked separately.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, we provide private airport transfer service for $45 per vehicle (accommodates up to 3 guests with luggage). Book when confirming your reservation.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. 50% refund for cancellations 15-29 days before arrival. No refund within 14 days of arrival. We recommend travel insurance.

Are children welcome?

Sound Temple is designed as an adult retreat focused on personal growth and creative work. We welcome guests 16 years and older. For family stays, please contact us to discuss options.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Our kitchen accommodates all dietary needs including vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, allergies, and religious preferences. Let us know your requirements when booking.

Is WiFi available?

Yes, high-speed fiber WiFi is available throughout the property. All rooms have dedicated work desks. We also have quiet co-working spaces for focused work.

What's the best time to visit?

Bali is beautiful year-round. Dry season (April-October) offers sunny days, ideal for outdoor activities. Wet season (November-March) brings lush greenery and fewer crowds. Both seasons have their charm.

Can I extend my stay?

Yes, subject to availability. Contact us as early as possible to extend your reservation. We offer discounts for stays longer than 7 nights.
