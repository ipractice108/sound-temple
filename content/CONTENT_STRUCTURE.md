## TONE & STYLE GUIDE

### Language Principles
- Clear and concise
- Professional yet warm
- Avoid hype words
- Focus on benefits
- Use active voice
- Short sentences preferred

### Words to Use
- Transform, create, discover
- Sanctuary, haven, space
- Authentic, sustainable, mindful
- Experience, journey, practice

### Words to Avoid
- Revolutionary, game-changing
- Best, ultimate, perfect
- Life-changing (use "transformative")
- Luxury overuse (show, don't tell)

### Call-to-Action Examples
- Book Your Stay
- Explore Experiences
- Start Your Journey
- Learn More
- Reserve Now
- Contact Us
- Join Our Community
