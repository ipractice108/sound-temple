Cacao Ceremony at the Mountain Tipi

Sacred cacao ritual with panoramic valley views.

Description

The cacao ceremony is an ancient practice rooted in the traditions of Central and South America.

Together we drink warm ceremonial cacao, follow a guided ritual, meditate, and connect with intention.

The experience concludes with a gong sound overlooking the valley — a moment of clarity, presence, and openness.

About Ceremonial Cacao

Cacao is considered a heart-opening medicine that helps release emotional blockages and connect with deeper wisdom. Unlike chocolate, ceremonial cacao is minimally processed and contains powerful compounds that enhance meditation and inner reflection.

The Ceremony Flow

  • Opening circle and intention setting
  • Ceremonial cacao preparation and serving
  • Guided meditation and breathwork
  • Silent reflection time
  • Sound healing with gong and singing bowls
  • Integration and sharing circle
  • Closing overlooking the valley

Benefits

  • Heart opening and emotional release
  • Enhanced meditation and inner clarity
  • Connection with intention and purpose
  • Community bonding in sacred space
  • Natural energy boost and mood elevation
  • Stress relief and relaxation

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothing for sitting
  • Journal and pen for reflections
  • Open heart and clear intention
  • Light stomach (avoid heavy meals 2 hours before)
