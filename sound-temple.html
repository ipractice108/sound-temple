Sound Temple

Description

A unique sound practice inside a fully mirrored dome with three resonance levels.

The Sound Temple is designed as a spatial instrument — a dome with three acoustic lenses that create deep, layered sound vibrations.

Inside, sound becomes tangible. Reflections amplify resonance, guiding the body and mind into a state of deep relaxation and inner balance.

This is a space where nature meets future architecture, and sound becomes a tool for transformation.
